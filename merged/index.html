How it works

  1. It reads the repo's last 10–20 merged PRs to learn its conventions.
  2. It runs the repo's CI commands locally and scores its own PR on 10 dimensions.
  3. It opens a draft and marks it ready for review only when its own score reaches 80%. By default, its PRs say they were opened with superhuman.
