      How it works

      1. It reads the repo's last 10–20 merged PRs to learn its conventions.
      2. It runs the repo's own CI and scores its own PR on 10 dimensions.
      3. It stops and explains instead of pushing a weak PR. By default, its PRs say they were opened with superhuman.

      MIT licensed and free.
